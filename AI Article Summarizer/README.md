# AI Article Summariser

## Usecase
AI Article Summariser is a Chrome extension designed to help readers, researchers, and busy professionals quickly digest long articles, essays, and news stories. By extracting page content and calling the Gemini API directly from the browser, it lets users grasp main themes and key findings in seconds, boosting productivity and reading efficiency.

## Problem Statement
The internet is flooded with long-form content, reports, and news updates. Reading every article in detail to evaluate its relevance is extremely time-consuming. Traditional copy-pasting into external chat portals interrupts the reading flow, and standard reading modes do not provide custom, structured formats like concise bullet points or detailed breakdowns.

## Features
- **One-Click Page Extraction**: Automatically extracts the text content of the active article or page paragraphs.
- **Multiple Summary Modes**: Choose between a *Brief Summary* (2-3 sentences), a *Detailed Summary* (covering all main arguments), or a *Bullet Points* list (5-7 key insights).
- **Fast Gemini Integration**: Integrates directly with the Gemini API for low-latency, state-of-the-art summarization.
- **Clipboard Integration**: Easily copy the generated summary to your clipboard with one click.
- **Synced Settings Configuration**: Stores your personal Gemini API key securely in synced storage across your signed-in browsers.

## How to Use
1. **Set up API Key**:
   - Right-click the extension icon and select **Options** (or open settings).
   - Enter your Gemini API key from Google AI Studio and click **Save Settings**.
2. **Summarise Articles**:
   - Navigate to any online article or web page you wish to summarise.
   - Click the extension icon in the toolbar.
   - Choose your preferred summary type (Brief, Detailed, or Bullet Points) from the dropdown.
   - Click **Summarize This Page**.
3. **Copy & Share**:
   - Once the summary is rendered, click the **Copy Summary** button to save it to your clipboard.
